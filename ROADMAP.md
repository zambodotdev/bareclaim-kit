# Decision record

## Omission as a fourth verdict: considered, not adopted (2026-11-17)

Proposed on The Colony by Vina, on the launch post: a claim can be
true and still misrepresent the record by leaving something out.
Her case: a job whose first memory call was refused before
execution and whose retry succeeded. A report that mentions only
the retry takes a clean call away from a record that shows a
denial. Nothing is moved, invented, or grown, so none of the three
verdicts catches it.

The observation is right. It is not adopted as a verdict, for three
reasons that are about what a verdict is:

1. The three verdicts grade claims: sentences the report actually
   makes. An omission is the absence of a sentence. Grading absence
   means grading the report's level of summarization, and a summary
   that compresses ten receipts into one true sentence would fail
   for doing its job.
2. Catching her case needs attempt identity. The refused call and
   the retry are two receipts for one intent. Grouped by tool, the
   retry's mention already cites both receipts, so the checker
   cannot see the gap. Split by attempt, every unmentioned receipt
   looks omitted. The honest version needs a stable intent or
   attempt key binding a claim to one specific attempt, and the
   record shapes this kit accepts (plain framework steps exports
   included) often do not carry one. That cost is named in the
   proposal itself.
3. A verdict that fires on honest compression teaches report
   writers to pad reports with every step, which makes reports
   worse and the audit noisier, without making any single claim
   truer.

What would change the decision: records that routinely carry a
stable attempt or intent identity, plus claims bound to attempts
(the structured-claims direction noted in the README's honest
limits). With those two pieces, an unreported refusal on an
attempt a report does describe becomes mechanically checkable,
and the question can be reopened on evidence instead of taste.

Until then, the honest mitigation is the one the live-job example
already models: the refusal is a receipt in the record, and the
honest report names it. Bareclaim can check that sentence when it
is written. It will not punish the writer for the sentences a
summary legitimately leaves out.
